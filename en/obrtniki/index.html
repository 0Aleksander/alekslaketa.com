VprašanjaQuestions

Pogovoriva se ↓Let's talk ↓
Bo »robot« odgovarjal mojim strankam namesto mene?Will a robot answer my customers instead of me?

Asistent odgovarja samo na vprašanja, za katera ste mu dali odgovor: delovni čas, območje, kjer delate, roki montaže. Prvi teden ne pošlje ničesar sam, samo pripravi osnutke. Ko ste zadovoljni, sme sam odgovarjati na vprašanja, ki ste jih odobrili. Vse drugo vedno počaka, da potrdite vi.Only questions you've given it an answer for: opening hours, the area you cover, installation dates. The first week it sends nothing on its own, it only drafts. Once you're happy, it may answer the questions you've approved by itself. Everything else always waits for you.

Kaj pa, če stranka vpraša za ceno?What if a customer asks for a price?

Cen asistent ne ugiba. Stranki napiše, da ji ponudbo pripravite vi, povpraševanje pa pride na vrh vašega seznama.The assistant never guesses prices. It tells the customer you'll prepare the quote yourself, and the enquiry goes to the top of your list.

Ali se moram naučiti nov program?Do I have to learn a new program?

Ne. Nič zapletenega se vam ni treba učiti: na telefon dobite en seznam in odgovore potrdite kar tam.No. You get one list on your phone and approve answers right there.

Kje so podatki mojih strank?Where is my customers' data?

Ostanejo vaši. Kjer se le da, izberem strežnike v EU. Pred začetkom podpiševa pogodbo o obdelavi osebnih podatkov, na vašo željo pa podatke izbrišem.It stays yours. Wherever I can, I choose servers in the EU. Before we start we sign a data processing agreement, and I delete it whenever you ask.

Kaj pa, če sodelovanje ne bo delovalo?What if it doesn't work out?

Vse postavim na vaše uporabniške račune in zapišem, kako deluje. Če nehava sodelovati, vse ostane vaše in deluje naprej.I set everything up on your own accounts and write down how it works. If we stop working together, it all stays yours and keeps running.

Ali moram zamenjati orodja, ki jih že uporabljam?Do I have to switch the tools I already use?

Ni nujno. Najprej pogledava, kaj že uporabljate, in pogosto je dovolj, da to povežem.Not necessarily. First we look at what you already use, and often it's enough for me to connect it.

Koliko stane?How much does it cost?

Enkrat plačate postavitev, potem mesečno vzdrževanje. Znesek je odvisen od tega, koliko kanalov poveževa, zato ga ne povem na pamet. Ko vidim, kaj imate, vam ceno pošljem pisno, preden kar koli začnem, in potem se odločite vi. Prvi pogovor ne stane nič.You pay once for the setup, then a monthly fee for upkeep. The amount depends on how many channels we connect, so I don't guess it. Once I've seen what you have, I send you the price in writing before I start anything, and then you decide. The first call costs nothing.

Kaj pa, če mi ne morete pomagati?What if you can't help me?

To vam povem že v prvem pogovoru.I'll tell you on the first call.

Delate samo na Goriškem?Do you only work around Gorica?

Ne. Kjer koli v Sloveniji se slišiva po telefonu ali prek videoklica, na Goriškem pa se dobiva tudi na kavi.No. Anywhere in Slovenia we talk by phone or video call, and around Gorica I'll come for coffee too.
